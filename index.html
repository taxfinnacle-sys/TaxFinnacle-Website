Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Who needs to file an Income Tax Return (ITR)?+

In India, you must file an Income Tax Return (ITR) if your total income exceeds the basic exemption limit or if you meet specific high-value or entity criteria. You can check rules and file through the Income Tax Department portal.

1. Income Above Basic Exemption Limit New Tax Regime: Total income above ₹4 lakh. Old Tax Regime: Total income above ₹2.5 lakh (under 60 years), ₹3 lakh (ages 60–80), or ₹5 lakh (80+ years).

2. High-Value Transactions & Specific Triggers Even if your income is below the exemption limit, filing is mandatory if you meet conditions such as: Foreign Assets or Income: Holding foreign assets/accounts, having signing authority in an overseas account, or earning foreign income. High Expenses: Spending over ₹2 lakh on foreign travel or over ₹1 lakh on electricity bills. High Deposits or Turnover: Large bank deposits (such as over ₹1 crore in a current account, over ₹50 lakh in savings deposits, or ₹5 crore+ in bank deposits depending on the rule) or business/professional turnover exceeding prescribed thresholds (such as ₹60 lakh for business or ₹10 lakh for profession). TDS/TCS Thresholds: Total TDS/TCS exceeding ₹25,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens).

3. Entities & Other Cases Entities: Companies, firms, HUFs, AOPs, and BOIs (as applicable under Section 263 of the Income Tax Act, 2025). Refunds & Losses: Filing is also needed to claim a tax refund or carry forward losses.

What documents are needed for ITR filing?+

Typically: PAN card, Aadhaar, Form 16 (for salaried), bank statements, investment proofs (80C, 80D), Form 26AS, AIS statement, and details of any other income. Our team will give you a tailored checklist once you get in touch.

What is the due date for GST return filing?+

GSTR-1 is due on the 11th of the following month (monthly filers). GSTR-3B is due on the 20th for monthly filers. Quarterly filers under QRMP have different deadlines. We track all deadlines for our clients and ensure zero late fees.

How long does company registration take?+

Private Limited company registration typically takes 7–15 working days depending on MCA processing. LLP and OPC follow similar timelines. We handle the entire process — DIN, DSC, name approval, MoA/AoA, and Certificate of Incorporation.

Can Tax Finnacle handle income tax notices?+

Absolutely. We assist with responding to notices under Sections 143(1), 139(9), 148, and scrutiny assessments. Don't ignore a tax notice — contact us immediately and our team will handle it professionally.
